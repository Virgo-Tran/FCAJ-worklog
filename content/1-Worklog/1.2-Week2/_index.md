### Week 2: Core infrastructure: IAM, VPC, EC2, S3, RDS

**Duration:** 12/05/2026 - 18/05/2026

### Objectives:

* Establish account governance with budgets and least-privilege IAM
* Build the core network and compute layer of a workload
* Learn how to store data and keep a running application under observation
