### [1. Optimizing Amazon EKS Costs: From Bill Shock to 40-60% Savings](3.1-Blog1/)
**Category:** *Container Infrastructure Cost Optimization*  
An in-depth analysis of the true cost structure of Amazon EKS clusters, explaining why CPU and memory over-provisioning is so widespread in DevOps, and presenting a 7-step roadmap to reduce EKS bills by 40-60% without compromising system reliability.

### [2. Lambda, ECS Fargate, EKS, or EC2 — Which One Should You Actually Use?](3.2-Blog2/)
**Category:** *AWS Compute Decision Framework*

### [3. AWS Lambda Durable Functions: Building Reliable Workflows Without Step Functions](3.3-Blog3/)
