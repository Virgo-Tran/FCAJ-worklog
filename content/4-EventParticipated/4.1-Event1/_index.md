# Summary Report: FCAJ Community Day

* **Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
* **Organizers:** AWS Study Group / FCAJ Organizers (Huynh Hoang Long, Thien Lu, Tran Dai Vi)

### Event Objectives

* Connect and network with fellow interns and cloud engineers in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program at AWS Vietnam.
* Stay updated on cutting-edge technical trends: AI/ML, Serverless Workflows, Multi-Agent Systems, and Cloud Infrastructure Optimization.
* Learn practical insights from industry experts and hear real-world stories of building products under pressure from hackathon teams.

---

### Key Highlights

#### 1. Context Is Everything: Making AI Actually Work for You (Speaker: Tinh Truong)
* Clarified why AI models often fail without proper context and defined what "context" truly means in production applications.
* Discussed the shift from prompt engineering to long-term memory management — introducing the **Second AI Brain** concept.
* Offered practical mindsets and advice for students to start building AI-driven products early.

#### 2. Friendly AI Assistant with Amazon Q (Speaker: Anh Pham)
* Introduced the Amazon Q assistant ecosystem:
  * **Quick Chat Agent:** AI assistants for data exploration and insight generation.
  * **Quick Flows:** Creating intelligent automated workflows with natural language without coding.
  * **Quick Spaces:** Collaborative spaces transforming individual insights into team knowledge.
  * **QuickSight:** Building data visualization dashboards from raw datasets via natural language.

#### 3. From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation (Speaker: Thinh Nguyen)
* Highlighted Amazon CloudFront as a foundational layer for all workloads (web, media, API).
* Explored cost optimization strategies, security capabilities (WAF & Shield integration), and enhanced performance and reliability at the edge.

#### 4. 36 hrs with LotusHacks – Building UTMorpho from Idea to Reality (Speaker: Team VIB)
* *Watch presentation and product demo:* **[YouTube Video - UTMorpho Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjMCrcDRACQ)**
* Shared Team VIB's 36-hour sprint at LotusHacks Hackathon: from initial brainstorming and defining real-world problems to non-stop development under tight deadlines.
* Presented **UTMorpho** product overview, live feature demo, key challenges, failures, and turning points.

#### 5. Non-Determinism of "Deterministic" LLM Settings (Speaker: Duc Dao)
* Deep dive into token selection mechanics in Large Language Models (LLMs).
* Debunked the myth that setting `Temperature = 0` guarantees 100% determinism, explaining the impact of inference optimizations on GPU hardware.
* Proposed concrete mitigation strategies for applications requiring strict output consistency.

#### 6. Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent System: The Case of Startup Credit Scoring (Speaker: Vy Lam)
* Analyzed the structural data mismatch between traditional banking systems and startup credit profiles.
* Compared Single-Agent vs. Multi-Agent paradigms.
* Presented the blueprint of a **Virtual Credit Committee** utilizing Multi-Agent architectures, guardrails & compliance enforcement, and operational ROI calculations.

---

### Key Takeaways

* **Deeper Understanding of AI & LLMs:** Recognized the critical role of Context Management and non-determinism challenges in real-world LLM deployments.
* **Edge Infrastructure Mindset:** Understood how Amazon CloudFront balances edge security, performance, and cost optimization.
* **Hackathon & Product Mindset:** Inspiring lessons from Team VIB on teamwork, resilience under pressure, and delivering a working prototype in 36 hours.
